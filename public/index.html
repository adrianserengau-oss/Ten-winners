How it works

Of the total amount accumulated in each draw 10% will be used to organize the game. The remaining 90% is split equally between the 10 winners.

If fewer than 10 tickets are sold, the draw is cancelled and every ticket is refunded.

Fair draw: before tickets go on sale, a secret seed is locked in. Its fingerprint is shown below and the seed is revealed after the draw, so anyone can check the result.
