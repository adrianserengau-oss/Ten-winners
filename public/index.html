    The 90/10 Split

    90% of ticket proceeds form the eligible prize pool, shared equally by 10 winners. The other 10% supports running and improving Ten Winners.

    How the draw works

    10 tickets are drawn at random. Your chance is the probability that at least one of your tickets is drawn. If fewer than 10 tickets are sold, the draw is cancelled and refunds are queued.

    A secret seed is locked in before ticket sales. Its fingerprint appears below, and the seed is revealed after the draw so anyone can check the result.
